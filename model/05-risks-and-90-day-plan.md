## 3. First Kaggle notebook: `e1-evolve-pilot`
Proposed kernel `aefinityaiinc/e1-evolve-pilot` (P-E), built from the proven `m5b-bitnet-qat-smoke` and `e16-bitnet-qat-ft` recipe (ledger M17 via P-E): transformers >= 5.x native BitNet, `trust_remote_code` off, plain `Trainer`, `pad_token = eos`, fp32 weights + fp16 AMP (T4 has no bf16), gradient checkpointing, `paged_adamw_8bit`, `device_map` auto over 2xT4, seq 1024, internet ON for pip and the HF model.
- **Purpose:** resolve the three Tier-0 unknowns (real tokens/s and the quota, whether operator behaviour moves into the weights, whether periphery-only tuning is enough at 2B) and prove the export chain before any money is spent. It is not a ship step.
- **Trains:** the bf16 master `microsoft/bitnet-b1.58-2B-4T-bf16` with online BitLinear QAT (file 02 S3).
- **On what:** a private Kaggle dataset of about 5-20M tokens of S1 episodes with construction labels from the synthetic worlds (B1-B7), frozen with SHA-256 in the manifest and **tokenized through the engine-port (G1)**; a **600-item dev slice disjoint from training** (about 40-60 items per bucket, so per-bucket intervals are wide). Public-pool probing (S2 on NQ etc.) is deferred to Notebook 2 on the winning arm.
- **Runs** (resumable, checkpoint every 100 steps, 12 h session limit): arm **P** periphery-only (all ternary weights frozen; embeddings, norms, SubLN gains trainable), arm **L** full QAT at LR 3e-5, arm **H** full QAT at LR 1e-4 (E16's value); cosine, warmup 10, WD 0, assistant-only loss, batch 1 x accum 16 (16,384 tokens/step). Budget 10-20M tokens per arm = 3.1-6.2 h per arm at the nominal 894 tok/s [arith], so three arms fit in about two sessions.
- **Cells in order:** (0) environment, dataset hash check, Kaggle quota meter and `nvidia-smi` logged; (1) **numerics check (G12):** fp16 on T4 vs a CPU bf16 reference on 20 prompts, gate: no non-finite values and first-token argmax agreement on >= 18 of 20 [plan GATE; no source supplies the 18]; (2) **baseline in the same harness:** the unmodified incumbent on the dev slice under P0 and T2, held-out PPL on a fixed 200-sequence slice, p(0) census against 0.42; (3) arms P, L, H; (4) E16-style export (unpacked int8 {-1,0,+1} plus F32 `_scale` siblings, 210 tensors) with a manifest of SHA-256s and markers `E1_TRAIN_PASS` / `E1_EXPORT_PASS`.
- **Must log, per checkpoint:** loss; **real** tokens/s on packed 1024-token sequences (not the nominal 894); quota meter before and after the session (settles 6 h vs 30 h); trit-flip fraction per tensor and the p(0) census; the HF-side greedy scorer on the dev slice **by bucket with Wilson intervals** under P0 and T2 (scorer parity-checked against `score.py` rules on 50 items); held-out PPL on the fixed 200-sequence slice; the arm name, LR and data hash.
- **Not done in the notebook:** engine receipts, CIS-full, max-residual-bits, boot. They run on box1/box2 after download (streaming fetch trick for outputs).
- **Success is judged by these decision gates (GATES, not predictions):**
  1. tokens/s measured and the quota logged;
  2. loss decreases with no NaN;
  3. the best arm's P0 exact-key `unknown_fact` rate on the dev slice is **>= 73.3%** (the incumbent's measured T2 value, 22/30, LAB-07 s8; reaching it with zero in-prompt demonstrations is non-trivial) **and** its `distractor` no-tool precision is not below the incumbent's value measured in the same dev harness at cell (2);
  4. held-out PPL ratio vs the incumbent <= 1.05 for the arm that passes gate 3 (flag above 1.02);
  5. step-0 export parity passes on a 50-sequence slice (PPL gap <= 3%, token parity);
  6. recorded, not gated: flip fraction per arm and whether arm P captures most of the gain (the ledger M26 14M result tested at 2B).
- **If any gate fails:** fix the data or the arm before S3 scales; no money is spent. Gate 3 on a 600-item slice is a movement test (disjoint Wilson intervals against the baseline), not a ship test.
