## 0. How to read this ledger
- One row per source. Columns: name, purpose, size, licence, commercial use, redistribution of derived weights, verified-by-gateway (yes/no), status.
- **Status values.** **verified in brief** = a brief (R1-R9) records reading that exact item's licence or card and no open legal question changes the yes/no. **unverified** = not read in any brief, or conditional, or an open legal question (share-alike reach to weights; ODC-By s4.3 "Produced Work"; Common Crawl indemnity; generator licence unread; lineage). **own work** = Aefinity-authored; the repo licence was read (Apache-2.0, `alice-aegis/LICENSE` [checked]); no third-party licence applies.
- **Audit column (2026-10-06).** `[verified: URL]` means the licence statement in the row was read at that primary source and agrees (after the corrections shown); it does not settle open legal questions. `[UNVERIFIED]` means no primary source was reached, sources conflict, or the claim rests on a brief alone. Struck-through text (~~like this~~) was wrong or stale. See Section M.
- R6 (data, licences) and R8 (eval sets) were **not** re-verified by R0-V (it covered R1-R4 only). A "verified in brief" row therefore means "the brief read it", not "an adversarial second agent confirmed it".
- **Verified-by-gateway = yes** means the label, argument and tool output are produced or checked by the real receipt gateway (`agent_trace`: generate -> execute -> trace receipt -> verify), and an example is kept only if it round-trips and the gateway output equals an independently computed truth. It says nothing about licence.
- Every training sample carries a **generator field** and enters a receipt-chained manifest (R6 s12 item 3; ledger M8 via P-E; G10). Decontamination (Qwen2.5 LCS >= 13 tokens and >= 0.6 of min length, plus 8-gram overlap >= 50%; R8 s7 G0.1) runs over ALL training data against every eval item, and removal counts are published.
- Sizes are the source's own unless marked. Token counts are in each dataset's tokenizer; the engine's 50,256-id ASCII vocabulary will count differently, and non-ASCII documents are filtered before any budget is final (R6 header).
