## Short version

- 22 claim statements were read out of the repo. 16 of them got a full prior-art hunt. **None came back "novel". All 16 are "partially-novel"**: what survives is a composition, a conformance protocol or assurance evidence, not a new primitive.
- Status of those 16 after the repo-evidence check: **8 done-in-repo, 2 in-progress, 6 planned, 0 speculative**. One status was corrected downward (row 9, from done-in-repo to in-progress).
- 6 claim statements carry no prior-art result (3 done-in-repo, 1 in-progress, 2 speculative). They are in the "Unverifiable" section, not the main table. Four of them restate or overlap main-table rows.
- The strongest surviving claim (row 1) is shown only under QEMU/OVMF + swtpm, on the small M7 test model, with a transient attestation key. No physical TPM, no Secure Boot, no EK certificate.
- Several statements in the repo's research briefs must be withdrawn or narrowed. See "Things we thought were new but are not".

## How this list was built

1. **Three readers** each took a share of the repo (research briefs, lab write-ups, specs, design docs, README) and pulled out every "nobody has done this" or "we did this first" statement, with the repo paths that support it. That gave 22 claim statements, which overlap in places.
2. **Prior-art hunt per claim** (16 of the 22). Web search where the search budget allowed, GitHub repository, code and issue search, arXiv, Hugging Face papers, IETF datatracker, crates.io and docs.rs, and direct reads of primary sources. Each hunt produced the nearest prior art and how close it is, a verdict (novel, partially-novel or not-novel), the precise remaining delta, and a narrowed claim. Several passes lost general web search to a shared budget. See "Search coverage" below.
3. **Repo-evidence check per claim** (all 22). A checker opened the cited repo paths, decided whether they support the claim as worded, and assigned a corrected status.
4. **Spot-check for this file (2026-10-06).** The cited repo paths exist. The two `RECEIPT.TXT` files hash to `528e205e04882437269fb1fbc6181ba0f2f6fddc120ba25f93c0e5bf233fbaaf` and compare byte-identical with `cmp`. Per-shape ratios in the VNNI logs and the per-repeat digests in the thread log were re-read. No build, test or boot was re-run.

**Honesty rule.** Every row shows the nearest prior art we found and the precise delta. "Novel" would mean the hunt found nothing close, "partially-novel" that parts exist, "not-novel" that the claim exists. No claim came back wholly not-novel, so the section "Things we thought were new but are not" lists the sub-claims and brief statements that failed. Absence claims are search results, not proofs.

**Status vocabulary.** `done-in-repo`: artifacts for the claim as narrowed exist in this repo (code, logs or a spec). It does not mean independently reproduced. `in-progress`: partly built or partly evidenced. `planned`: a design or roadmap exists, with no code or logs. `speculative`: a design position with no plan and no evidence. Statuses below are the corrected ones from the evidence check.
